VŠE FIS · TEAM CASE STUDY

Student Overreliance on AI

This case study explores how AI can support students' learning while also creating a risk that it replaces their own attempts to solve problems. The project focuses on overreliance, not clinical addiction.

Approach

The team combined an anonymous questionnaire among first-year FIS students with external research. The analysis used a Fishbone Diagram, 5 Whys, comparisons of solution options, and a risk matrix.

Developed as a team project for the Problem Solving course at VŠE, the case study brought together research, structured problem analysis, and the evaluation of practical interventions. Working as a team helped us consider different perspectives and turn the analysis into a practical proposal.

Proposed direction: AI Study Smart

The recommended approach encourages students to Try → Hint → Solve → Verify: attempt the task independently, request hints rather than a finished answer, solve it in their own words, and verify the result. The proposal combines a short study guide, a practical workshop, and assessment tasks that check independent understanding.
